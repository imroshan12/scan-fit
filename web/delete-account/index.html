Delete your Mr Doc account

Mr Doc by Sarvesh Roshan

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mr Doc and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account › Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email pro.sarvesh.roshan@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Mr Doc account". We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and account ID.
  • Your free-save count. If you sign in again later, you start a new account.

What is not deleted

  • Files on your phone. Mr Doc never had them. Delete them yourself, or uninstall the app to remove what it stored on the phone.
  • Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a yearly plan. Cancel it in Google Play or App Store settings (how).
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat, for as long as tax and other laws require.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics that are not linked to your account. They are deleted on their normal schedule (up to 90 days for crash reports, up to 14 months for statistics).
