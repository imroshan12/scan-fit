Saving files

Where are my files saved?

On Android, in Downloads › Mr Doc › (exam name). On iPhone, Files opens and you choose the folder. Uploading straight from Photos can change a file on some websites, so pick it from Files or Downloads when you upload.

The portal rejected my file. What now?

First, check the exam's current notice: rules change. Then open the file with Check a file to see its size, pixels and format. If Mr Doc made the file for that exam and the portal still refused it, tell us with Settings › Report a problem (it sends the exam name and app version, never your file) and we will check the rules.

Why is my file bigger than the picture needs?

Some portals set a minimum size, such as 10 KB for a signature. Mr Doc fills a small file up to that minimum so the portal accepts it. That's on purpose.

Free saves and Pro

What counts as a save?

A new file you save or share from the app. Saving or sharing the same file again, keeping a picture in My Kit and checking a file are free. A save that fails doesn't count.

What happens after 20 saves?

You can still use every tool, preview your files and check them. To save or share more, get Pro: ₹99 a year or ₹199 once. Your work is kept, so you can save it right after buying.

I bought Pro but the app still asks me to pay.

Make sure you are signed in with the same account you bought with, then go to Settings › Pro › Restore purchases. If it still doesn't work, write to us with the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

On Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Mr Doc › Cancel. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Mr Doc › Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through Google Play or Apple, so they handle refunds. On Android, request one from your Google Play order history; on iPhone, at reportaproblem.apple.com. Write to us if you need help.

Your account and privacy

Why do I need to sign in?

Your account keeps your free-save count and Pro, so they stay with you when you reinstall or change phones. It holds only your email, your name and how many saves you have used. Your files never go to your account.

Do you see my documents?

No. Everything is processed on your phone. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. Without the app, see delete your account.

Is Mr Doc an official app?

No. Mr Doc is an independent tool, not affiliated with any exam body or government. Exam rules come from official notifications, linked in the app.
