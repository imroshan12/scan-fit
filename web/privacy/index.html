Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version
  • Your photos, signatures, documents and PDFs are processed on your phone and never uploaded to us.
  • Your account (Google or Apple sign-in) holds only your email, your name and how many free saves you have used.
  • We use crash reports and usage statistics that never contain your files, file names or text, and you can turn the statistics off.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Mr Doc is made by Sarvesh Roshan, an individual developer in Gurugram (Gurgaon), Haryana, India ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to pro.sarvesh.roshan@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you capture or open in Mr Doc stays on your device: photos, signatures, thumb impressions, handwritten pages, certificates, PDFs, the files you make, their names, the exams you pick or pin, your saved originals ("My Kit") and your save history. Mr Doc processes them on the phone itself and never sends them to us or to any of our service providers.

A file leaves your phone only when you choose to save it to a folder or share it with another app. What happens after that is up to you and the app you share with.

Mr Doc asks for the camera only when you take a photo in the app, and reads only the photos and files you pick.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads and we don't use the advertising ID.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't upload, read or analyse your documents.

Where your data is kept

Our service providers may process the data above on servers outside India, including in the United States. They act on our instructions and protect the data in transit with encryption.

How long we keep it

  • Account and save count: until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records: as long as the stores, RevenueCat and the law (for example tax rules) require.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Settings › Account › Delete account) or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Turn off usage statistics in Settings › Privacy.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of the data we hold about you, or raise a complaint, by writing to pro.sarvesh.roshan@gmail.com. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we don't resolve your complaint.
  • Your files are yours: delete them from your phone, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored there.

Children and teenagers

Mr Doc is for people preparing documents and forms, including students preparing for entrance exams. It is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, use Mr Doc with the permission and help of a parent or guardian.

Security

Data travels between the app and our providers over encrypted connections. Your account record can be read and changed only by you, through the app, and only the save count can change, and only upwards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change matters for your data, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
