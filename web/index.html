Questions

Is Mr Doc an official government or exam app?

No. Mr Doc is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any exam body or government. Exam rules come from official notifications, linked in the app. Always check the current notice before you submit.

Do my photos or documents get uploaded?

No. Everything you capture is processed and stored on your phone. A file leaves your phone only when you save or share it yourself.

Why do I sign in?

Your Google or Apple account keeps your free-save count and your Pro purchase, so they stay with you when you change phones. The account holds only your email, your name and the number of saves you have used.
